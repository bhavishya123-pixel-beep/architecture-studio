## Research Question

What is the complete, currently-binding legal and regulatory framework that governs a Singapore-domiciled holding company channelling foreign and domestic capital into Indian real-estate development, redevelopment, and asset management vehicles?

This question must be answered with enough specificity to map every regulatory instrument to a named ENIGMA entity and to a specific set of activities that instrument enables or prohibits. Generic compliance overviews are not acceptable outputs.

---

## Instruments to Be Analysed

The following instruments must be reviewed in their current, in-force version as of the date of research. Where an instrument has been amended, the amendment history must be noted and the most recent amendment must control.

**Foreign Exchange and Capital Account**
- Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999 and all current RBI Master Directions issued thereunder
- FDI Policy Press Note 3 of 2020 (consolidated FDI policy) with all subsequent amendments
- RBI External Commercial Borrowing framework (Master Direction — External Commercial Borrowings, Trade Credits and Structured Obligations)
- RBI Master Direction on Foreign Investment in India (current version)

**Securities and Fund Regulation**
- SEBI (Alternative Investment Funds) Regulations, 2012, including all circulars and amendments through the date of research
- IFSCA (Fund Management) Regulations, 2022 — GIFT City IFSC AIF framework
- SEBI (Real Estate Investment Trusts) Regulations, 2014 with all amendments
- SEBI (Infrastructure Investment Trusts) Regulations, 2014 with all amendments

**Real Estate Sector**
- Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 (RERA) — central provisions
- Maharashtra RERA rules and regulations (MahaRERA)
- Delhi-NCR: DDA Act provisions and Haryana/UP state RERA rules as applicable
- Karnataka RERA rules and regulations (K-RERA)
- Telangana RERA rules and regulations (TSRERA)

**Taxation**
- Income Tax Act, 1961 — Section 45(5A) (joint development agreements), Section 54 (capital gains reinvestment), Section 54F (long-term capital asset reinvestment), Section 54EC (bond reinvestment)
- Finance Act amendments affecting GIFT City taxation — current regime for offshore fund managers operating from GIFT City IFSC
- Tax treaty between India and Singapore — current provisions relevant to dividend withholding, capital gains, and management fees
- GAAR provisions and their applicability to the Singapore HoldCo structure

**Corporate and Governance**
- Companies Act, 2013 — provisions applicable to Indian subsidiaries of foreign holding companies
- Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008 — provisions relevant to SPV structuring
- Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002 and its application to AIF fund managers

## Deliverable Format

A single structured document organised as a matrix with the following columns:

| Regulatory Instrument | Governing Authority | ENIGMA Entity Affected | Activity Enabled | Activity Prohibited or Constrained | Penalty / Consequence of Breach | Date of Last Amendment |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Below the matrix, a separate section for each ENIGMA entity (Singapore HoldCo, Enigma Certification, Enigma Realty, Enigma Living) summarising its regulatory exposure profile in plain language.

A final section flagging any instrument where the research team was unable to locate the current in-force version and is therefore relying on a version that may be outdated.

## Sources

All claims must be verified against the primary text of the instrument. Secondary summaries from law firms, consultancies, or news sources may be used to identify instruments but may not be cited as evidence of what an instrument says. The primary text controls in all cases.

Recommended primary source repositories:
- RBI website (rbi.org.in) — Master Directions and circulars
- SEBI website (sebi.gov.in) — regulations, circulars, and FAQs
- IFSCA website (ifsca.gov.in) — GIFT City framework documents
- Ministry of Finance / DPIIT (for FDI press notes)
- Income Tax India (incometaxindia.gov.in) — Act text and Finance Act amendments
- IndiaCode (indiacode.nic.in) — consolidated statute text
