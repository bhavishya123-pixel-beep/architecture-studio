# Module 05 — Competitor Intelligence

**Status:** `SCAFFOLDED`

**Consumed by:** Modules 06, 07, 08, 09

## Research Question

Where does structural arbitrage exist for a new entrant given the current competitive landscape — meaning: which high-growth segments are not being served by incumbents in ways that a focused, capital-disciplined, operationally differentiated platform can exploit?

The whitespace is not simply where incumbents are absent. It is where incumbents are structurally incapable of operating or are operating with the wrong model, and where ENIGMA's specific combination of Passive House certification, sponsor-economics structure, and senior living focus creates a defensible position.

---

## Entities to Be Profiled

**Listed REIT Platforms**

*Embassy Office Parks REIT*
- Capital structure: unit count, total debt, LTV, average cost of debt
- Sponsor economics: Embassy Group and Blackstone respective carried interest, management fee, and development pipeline fee arrangements
- Governance history: related-party transaction record, SEBI examination history, unitholders' meeting outcomes
- Regulatory exposure: SEBI REIT regulation compliance record
- Arbitrage not being captured: which segments, geographies, or tenant categories Embassy is not addressing

*Brookfield India REIT*
- Same four-point profile as Embassy
- Specific focus: Brookfield's global platform vs. its India REIT governance structure and whether parent-entity transfer pricing is visible in disclosed financials

*Mindspace Business Parks REIT*
- Same four-point profile
- Specific focus: K. Raheja Corp sponsor relationship and the pipeline-injection agreement economics

*Nexus Select Trust*
- Same four-point profile
- Specific focus: Blackstone's retail REIT experiment — the specific mechanism by which retail mall cash flows are being REITified and what this reveals about the depth of the Indian REIT exit market for non-office real estate

**Direct Platform**

*Blackstone India (direct, non-REIT)*
- What Blackstone holds in India outside its listed REITs: estimated AUM, asset categories, vintage
- The warehousing and logistics build-out through ESR and Horizon Industrial Parks
- The relationship between Blackstone's India direct platform and its BREP Asia funds — capital flows, reporting lines, fee structures where disclosed
- Where Blackstone India is not investing and why — this is the most important question in this profile

**Integrated Developer-Operators**

For each of Lodha (Macrotech Developers), DLF, Prestige Estates, Sobha, and Oberoi Realty, produce a profile covering:

1. *Capital structure*: net debt, ICR, cost of debt vs. average realization per sq ft, land bank financing vs. construction financing split
2. *Sponsor economics*: promoter shareholding, promoter remuneration, related-party land sales and their disclosed margins — the gap between promoter economics and minority shareholder economics
3. *Governance history*: NCLT proceedings, RERA complaints volume where disclosed, ED or CBI attention, listed entity vs. promoter entity transaction record
4. *Regulatory exposure*: RERA project registration compliance, NCLT creditor proceedings, SEBI disclosure compliance record
5. *Arbitrage not being captured*: which customer segments, price bands, or geographies these entities are structurally unable or unwilling to serve — Passive House, senior living, and net-zero are the primary lenses

## Deliverable Format

**Section A: Entity Profiles** — one subsection per entity in the format above.

**Section B: Competitive Whitespace Map** — a two-dimensional matrix plotting the ten market segments from Module 03 against the competitive intensity (number of well-capitalised, operationally competent incumbents) and the structural fit with ENIGMA's model. The three highest-conviction entry points must be identified and argued, not just listed.

**Section C: Build vs. Partner vs. Avoid** — for each high-conviction entry point, a one-page argument for whether ENIGMA should build independently, partner with an existing operator, or avoid the segment entirely. The argument must reference Module 02's replicability verdicts and Module 03's CAGR projections explicitly.

---

## This module is wrong if: the competitive whitespace identified in Section B is closed by an existing incumbent or new entrant within 24 months of this module's finalisation without ENIGMA having established a position, or if any profiled entity's capital structure, sponsor economics, or governance history is materially mischaracterised relative to what their publicly filed documents disclose.
