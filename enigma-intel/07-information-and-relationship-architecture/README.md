## Research Question

How does an emerging fund manager build a Goldman Sachs-grade relationship and information infrastructure — one that produces the deal flow, LP access, and market intelligence of an institutional platform — while operating entirely within legal and ethical bounds and without the political-favour networks that Indian developers above the ₹5,000 crore revenue mark have historically relied on?

This module is fundamentally about the design of an institutional moat that does not require regulatory risk to construct.

---

## Section A: The Two Models Contrasted

**The Institutional Capital-Allocator Model (Blackstone / Brookfield)**

Analyse in detail how Blackstone and Brookfield built their LP relationship infrastructure:
- The specific conference and investor-day circuit: PERE, ULI, MIPIM, IREI, Milken — what role each plays in the relationship-building sequence
- The role of placement agents vs. direct LP relationships: economics, regulatory constraints in India (SEBI intermediary registration requirements for placement agents raising from Indian LPs)
- The data room and reporting infrastructure: what institutional LPs require in terms of quarterly reporting, fund administrator independence, auditor reputation, and data room access standards
- How Blackstone maintains information advantages over LPs without breaching confidentiality obligations: market research publication, research report distribution, proprietary data partnerships

**The Promoter-Political-Favour Model**

Document with specificity how Indian developers above the ₹5,000 crore revenue mark have historically built relationships with regulators, politicians, and capital providers:
- The specific mechanisms: land acquisition facilitation, FSI approvals acceleration, banker relationships, media ownership
- This section must be documented with reference to public record — disclosed regulatory actions, court proceedings, journalist investigations — not alleged or inferred

## Section C: The ENIGMA Compliance Wall

A written document defining:

**What ENIGMA will do:**
- Build LP relationships through disclosed institutional channels only (conference circuit, introduced meetings, regulatory-compliant placement agents)
- Maintain a public-facing research publication programme to build information-based credibility
- Report to LPs on a quarterly basis using a format aligned with ILPA standards
- Retain a Big Four auditor and a SEBI-registered fund administrator from Fund I inception
- Maintain a SEBI-registered AIF management entity with full statutory compliance

**What ENIGMA will not do:**
- Engage with any person acting as an intermediary for regulatory approvals
- Accept capital from any LP whose source of funds cannot be verified to the standard of a scheduled commercial bank's KYC process
- Permit any ENIGMA employee or director to hold an interest in any vendor, contractor, or counterparty without full disclosure and board approval
- Use political relationships to accelerate regulatory approvals (RERA registrations, FSI sanctions, SEBI filings)

**The early warning system:**
- Specific metrics that would indicate ENIGMA is drifting toward the promoter-favour model
- The person responsible for monitoring each metric
- The escalation pathway when a metric triggers

## Section D: Operational Systems

**CRM Architecture**
- Contact categories: LPs (current and prospective), co-investors, brokers, regulators (arms-length only), research partners, media
- The data each category requires and the data each category may not receive
- The compliance log: every LP interaction must be logged with date, participants, content summary, and any material non-public information disclosure assessment

**Data Room Infrastructure**
- Minimum standards for LP data room: administrator identity, access control, versioning, audit trail
- The ILPA reporting template and how it maps to ENIGMA's fund structure
- How to structure a data room for a first-time fund that does not have three years of audited track record

**Conference and Research Circuit**
- The specific conferences worth attending at each stage of fund development
- The research report publication programme: what to publish, how often, and through what distribution mechanism to reach institutional LPs without triggering SEBI solicitation restrictions
